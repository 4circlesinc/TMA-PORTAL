Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TM ANTOINE Advisory (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use our client portal at portal.tmantoinelaw.com (the “Portal”). We are committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of everyone who uses the Portal.

1. Scope

This policy applies to information we process through the Portal, a private, invitation- and approval-based application used by TM ANTOINE Advisory and its clients to manage matters, documents, e-signatures, scheduling, messaging, and related communications. It does not cover any third-party website or service that we do not operate, even where the Portal links to it.

The controller is TM ANTOINE Advisory, TaylorMarc Court, Rodney Bay, Gros Islet, Saint Lucia. Email support@tmantoinelaw.com. We have not appointed a data-protection officer. We are established in Saint Lucia and have not appointed a representative in the UK or the EEA. If UK or EU data-protection law applies to you, write to us at the address above and we will handle the request. You may also complain to the data-protection authority where you live.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account & profile details: your name, email address, phone number, job title or organization, profile photo, and the password or sign-in method you use.
  • Content you submit: documents and files you upload, e-signatures and the information within signed records, calendar events, and messages you send through the Portal.
  • Communications: information you provide when you contact us for support or otherwise correspond with us.

Information we collect automatically

  • Usage & device data: log records such as IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, and actions taken, which we use to operate and secure the Portal.
  • Security data: sign-in events, two-factor authentication status, and trusted-device identifiers used to protect your account.

A citizenship or immigration file may also include identity documents, nationality, dates of birth, and family details, including for a child you are responsible for. We use that information because you asked us to prepare the filing, and because we have to keep the professional record of the matter.

3. Connected accounts & third-party data

You may sign in to the Portal with your Google or Microsoft account, and you may optionally connect that account to sync email, calendar, and cloud files (Google Drive, OneDrive, or SharePoint). When you do, we receive only the data covered by the permissions you grant, and only for the features you enable.

Google user data

The Portal’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

We access your Google data (such as your profile, and, only if you enable sync, your Gmail messages and Google Calendar events) solely to provide and improve the user-facing features you request within the Portal. We do not transfer this data to others except as needed to provide those features, for security purposes, or to comply with applicable law; we do not use it for advertising; and we do not allow humans to read it unless you give explicit consent, it is necessary for security or to comply with law, or the data is aggregated and de-identified.

Microsoft user data

When you connect a Microsoft account, we access your profile and, only if you enable sync, your Outlook mail, calendar, and OneDrive/SharePoint files via Microsoft Graph, solely to provide the features you request. We handle this data on the same limited-use basis described above.

You can review or revoke these connections at any time from your Security settings in the Portal, or directly with the provider at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or account.microsoft.com/privacy (Microsoft).

4. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and operate the Portal and its features;
  • Authenticate you, secure your account, and prevent fraud or abuse;
  • Deliver documents, e-signature requests, notifications, and messages;
  • Sync email, calendar, and files when you enable those connections;
  • Provide support and respond to your requests;
  • Maintain audit trails and comply with our legal and professional obligations; and
  • Improve the reliability, performance, and security of the Portal.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Lawful bases

Where the UK or EU General Data Protection Regulation applies, we rely on:

  • Contract: providing the Portal and the work you engaged us to do;
  • Legal obligation: professional, tax, and filing records we are required to keep;
  • Legitimate interests: securing accounts, preventing abuse, and keeping an audit of actions on a matter; and
  • Consent: optional email, calendar, and file sync with Google or Microsoft, only while that connection stays on. Disconnecting the account withdraws that consent. Signing in with Google or Microsoft is separate from sync.

5. How we share information

We share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: vendors who host and support the Portal under contract, including our cloud hosting, database, file storage, and email-delivery providers. They may process data only on our instructions.
  • Within your matter: with the TM ANTOINE Advisory personnel and authorized participants involved in your matter, as needed to provide our services.
  • Legal & safety: when required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our clients, our firm, or others.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or similar transaction, subject to this policy.

International transfers

We are in Saint Lucia. Our hosting, email, and identity providers, including Google and Microsoft when you connect them, may store and process information in the United States and other countries where they operate. Where UK or EU law applies and the destination country is not covered by an adequacy decision, the contract with that provider is what requires them to protect the information.

6. Storage, security & retention

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored using reputable cloud infrastructure with access controls, encrypted object storage, and audit logging. We support two-factor authentication and trusted-device management to help protect accounts. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information using appropriate safeguards.

We keep information only as long as the purpose requires:

  • Your activity history and read notifications: the period you choose in Settings → Privacy (7 to 365 days; 30 days if you have not chosen).
  • Sign-in history: two years, then deleted.
  • Call recordings: the firm’s security retention period (seven years unless a shorter period is set), or longer if a recording is on legal hold.
  • Your account profile: while the account is open. If you erase the account, the profile, sessions, and connected-account access are removed.
  • Matter files and citizenship records: for as long as the matter requires and for the professional retention period that applies to that file.
  • An erasure request: we keep a record of the request, including the email address it concerned, so we can show what was deleted and what was kept.

7. Your rights & choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or update your personal information;
  • Request deletion of certain information;
  • Disconnect connected Google or Microsoft accounts and revoke their permissions;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing; and
  • Request a copy of your information in a portable format.

Signed-in users can do this from Settings → Privacy:

  • Access and portability: download a copy of your account data.
  • Correction: update your name, email, and phone from Profile.
  • Restriction: stop optional Google and Microsoft sync. Work we are already engaged to do continues.
  • Erasure: close your login and delete your profile, sessions, and connected-account access. Matter files, messages, signatures, and citizenship records stay where a professional or legal duty requires them. Your name is removed from the account, and we email you a confirmation.
  • Connected accounts: disconnect Google or Microsoft from Security settings, or at the provider.

For anything those screens do not cover, email support@tmantoinelaw.com. We will respond consistent with applicable law and our professional obligations.

8. Cookies & sessions

The Portal uses strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in, remember trusted devices, and protect against cross-site request forgery. These are required for the Portal to function and are not used for advertising or cross-site tracking.

9. Children’s privacy

A Portal account is for adults. We do not let a child open their own account. A citizenship or immigration file may include a child you are responsible for. We collect that information only as part of the application, with the parental or guardian consent the filing requires. If you believe a child has an account of their own, contact us and we will close it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and notify you through the Portal. Where we rely on consent, including optional Google or Microsoft sync, we will ask again before using your information for a new purpose. Continued use of the Portal is not how we collect consent.
